How It Works

How It Works: Step-by-Step Cleaning Perfection

01.
Vehicle Inspection
We begin with a quick pre-wash inspection to identify dirt levels, paint condition attention.
02.
Rinse & Dirt Removal
Using high-pressure water jets, we rinse away loose dirt and debris from the car’s surface. 
03.
Foam Wash Application
A thick layer of eco-friendly foam shampoo is applied  to loosen grime and grease.
04.
Scrubbing
Our trained professionals hand-wash your car with microfiber mitts and pH-balanced soap.
05.
Wheel & Tire Cleaning
Specialized brushes & cleaners are used to remove brake dust, grease, and mud from car.
06.
 Rinse & Spot-Free Dry
After washing, we perform a spotless rinse, use microfiber towels or air blowers.
07.
Interior Cleaning
We vacuum seats, carpets, and dashboards, followed by sanitization and air freshening.
08.
Waxing & Polishing
A protective wax, polish is applied to enhance shine and guard against UV rays & dust.
09.
Final Inspection
Every car undergoes a final quality check before delivery to make sure it meet standard.
